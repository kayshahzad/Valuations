# Technology Sector Heuristics

### 1. Rule of 40
*   **Metric**: Revenue Growth % + EBITDA Margin %.
*   **Benchmark**: Should be > 40% for healthy SaaS companies.
*   **Context**: If < 40%, the company is either growing too slowly or burning too much cash.

### 2. Net Revenue Retention (NRR)
*   **Metric**: NRR.
*   **Benchmark**: > 110% is best-in-class; < 100% indicates churn issues.
*   **Context**: High NRR implies "Land and Expand" success.

### 3. Margins: Hardware vs. Software
*   **SaaS**: Gross Margins should be 70-80%+.
*   **Hardware**: Gross Margins typically 30-50% (e.g. Apple, Nvidia).
*   **Alert**: If a Software company has < 60% GM, check if they are hiding service costs (Professional Services).
### 4. Strategic Capitalization
*   **Capitalize**: Research and Development (R&D).
*   **Amortization Period**: 3 Years.
*   **Logic**: R&D is an investment in future growth, not just a current expense.

# Healthcare Sector Heuristics

### 1. Patent Cliffs
*   **Context**: Check expiration dates of key drugs. Revenue can drop 80% upon generic entry.
*   **Valuation Impact**: Terminal growth should be lower (or negative) for single-drug companies near cliff.

### 2. R&D Efficiency
*   **Metric**: R&D as % of Revenue.
*   **Benchmark**: 15-25% for Pharma/BioTech.
*   **Context**: If R&D is high but pipeline is weak, value destruction risk is high.

### 3. Regulatory Risk
*   **FDA Approval**: Binary outcomes. DCF should model probability-weighted scenarios for pre-revenue biotech (though Aletheia focuses on established cash flows).

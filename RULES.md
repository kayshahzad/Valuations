# Aletheia Constitution (Valuation Rules)

This document defines the immutable laws that govern all investment recommendations.

### 1. The Efficiency Floor
*   **Rule**: NO BUY recommendation is permitted if ROIC < WACC in Year 5.
*   **Logic**: A company destroying value in the terminal year cannot be a long-term compounder.

### 2. The Terminal Cap
*   **Rule**: Terminal Growth Rate (`g`) is hard-capped at **3.0%** for all sectors.
*   **Exception**: Unless `Moat Score` > 8 (Wide Moat), `g` can exceed 3% (up to WACC-0.5%).
*   **Logic**: Few companies outgrow the economy (GDP) forever.

### 3. The WACC Floor
*   **Rule**: Use a **9.0%** minimum WACC for all equities with Market Cap < $100B.
*   **Logic**: Accounts for liquidity risk and small-cap volatility often ignored by pure CAPM.

### 4. Agnostic Variance Check
*   **Rule**: All valuations must include an Agnostic Variance Check (Cyclicality & Intangibles).
*   **Mandate**: If "Cyclical Peak" is detected (Z-Score > 2.0), the Base Year for DCF must be normalized (3-Year Mean).
*   **Knowledge Assets**: If the business is "Knowledge Heavy" (e.g., Pharma, Tech), pipeline risks must be verified against Market Cap impact (Terminal Haircut).

### 5. Valuation Integrity
*   **Rule**: The Lead Agent must explicitly cite these rules in the "Constitution Check" section of the final report.
### 6. Efficiency Ratios (Dynamic Intelligence)
*   **Rule**: The Fundamentalist must analyze the following sector-specific ratios:
    *   **Retail**: Days Sales Outstanding (DSO), Inventory Turnover.
    *   **Hotels/REITs**: Occupancy Rate, REVPAR.
    *   **Healthcare Plans**: Medical Loss Ratio (MLR), Days Claims Payable.
    *   **Technology**: Rule of 40, Magic Number (Sales Efficiency).
    *   **Banking**: Efficiency Ratio (Non-Interest Exp / Revenue), Tier 1 Capital.
### 7. Sector Median ROIC (Benchmarks)
*   **Rule**: Compare company ROIC against these sector medians:
    *   **Technology**: 18.5%
    *   **Healthcare**: 12.0%
    *   **Consumer Staples**: 14.0%
    *   **Industrials**: 10.5%
    *   **Energy**: 9.0%
    *   **Financials**: 11.0% (ROE)
